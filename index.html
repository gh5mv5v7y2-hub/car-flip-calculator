WHAT IS FLIPGUARD?

A second opinion before your money leaves your pocket.

FlipGuard is a free deal-screening tool for people buying used cars to resell. It compares repair scenarios, estimated profit, ROI and downside risk, then gives you a suggested offer range and a separate confidence check on the inputs behind the deal.

FlipGuard is an estimation tool, not a mechanical inspection or guarantee of profit. Always verify a vehicle’s condition, title, mileage and market value before buying.
